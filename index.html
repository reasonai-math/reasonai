Curriculum and assessment notes

The questions are inspired by Year 3 DSKP strands: numbers, operations, fractions, money, time, measurement and space. Individual Olympiad-style extensions (notably Q7, Q11–Q15) deliberately go beyond routine curricular expectations; they should not be described as strictly mapped to each Year 3 learning standard without a formal item-level mapping exercise.

No factual claims are made about participant cohort size, prevalence of giftedness, improvement due to ReasonAI or national percentile ranks. All scores require teacher input and rubric moderation. Local browser records are not a secure central research repository. Use anonymous IDs and export data before clearing the browser or moving to another device.

Source references: KSSR Semakan 2017 Matematik Tahun 3 DSKP and curriculum alignment documents. Conduct expert content validation, pilot item analysis, accessibility checks and consent review before formal use.
